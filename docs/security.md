# Pousada Passárgada — Privacy and Operational Boundaries

## Not published

- Production source code
- Credentials and tokens
- Guest names, phone numbers and emails
- Reservation records
- Internal revenue reports
- Private commercial configuration
- Administrative access details

## Analytics

Marketing measurement should respect consent and avoid sending personally identifiable guest data in analytics events.

## Booking data

The public website should not expose booking-engine credentials or private booking records.

## Operational truth

Availability, inventory, rates, restrictions, payment and booking confirmation remain the responsibility of the operational booking system.

Any public screenshot or example must exclude personal and administrative information.
