# Pousada Passárgada — Digital Product Scope

## Context

The work sits inside an active hospitality operation. Product decisions are informed by guest questions, reservation behavior, campaigns, seasonality and the real operational limits of the property.

## Digital responsibilities

- Present accommodations and experiences clearly.
- Support direct-booking intent.
- Connect visitors to the operational booking engine.
- Preserve relevant legacy URLs during migration.
- Support campaign attribution.
- Improve search-engine discoverability.
- Keep public promises aligned with what operations can actually deliver.

## Booking boundary

```text
Website intent → Dates / occupancy / campaign context → OmniBees
→ Availability / rate / payment / confirmation
```

The website reduces friction without duplicating systems that already own inventory, pricing and confirmation.
